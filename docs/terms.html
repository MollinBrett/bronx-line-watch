Terms of Service

Effective November 1, 2026

Bronx Line Watch ("we", "us") is a free, non-commercial text message service that sends NYC subway service alerts for the 2, 4, 5, 6, B and D lines. By texting BRONX to [PHONE NUMBER] and replying YES, you agree to these terms.

The service

  • You'll receive text messages about real-time service disruptions (delays, suspensions, reroutes, and similar changes) on the lines above, in English or Spanish.
  • Message frequency varies with subway conditions. You may receive several messages on a disrupted day and none on a normal one.
  • The service is free. Message and data rates may apply according to your mobile plan.
  • We never send marketing or promotional messages.

Opting out and getting help

  • Reply STOP at any time to unsubscribe. You'll get one message confirming you've been removed, and no further messages.
  • Reply HELP for help, or visit our website.
  • Reply ESPANOL to switch to Spanish, or ENGLISH to switch back.

Accuracy

Alerts are based on the MTA's public service data and are rewritten in plain language by an automated system that uses an AI model. Messages may be delayed, incomplete, or occasionally inaccurate, and carriers do not guarantee delivery. Always check official MTA sources such as new.mta.info before making travel decisions. Bronx Line Watch is not affiliated with or endorsed by the MTA.

We may change or discontinue the service at any time; if we do, we'll try to tell subscribers by text first.
